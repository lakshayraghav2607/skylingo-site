Your trainer

Meet Your Trainer – Aakash Sharma

Aakash Sharma | English Language Trainer | Communication & Personality Development Coach

With over 7 years of experience in English language training and communication development, I have dedicated my professional journey to helping individuals overcome hesitation, discover their confidence, and become effective communicators.

I am a USA-certified International English Language Trainer and a 120-hour TEFL-certified professional. My expertise includes Spoken English, Business Communication, Public Speaking, Personality Development, Interview Preparation, and Professional Communication.

Throughout my journey, I have worked with students, aspiring professionals, and working individuals, helping them improve not just their English but also the way they express themselves, interact with others, and present their personalities.

My teaching philosophy goes beyond textbooks and grammar rules. I believe in practical learning, real-life conversations, interactive activities, and confidence-building experiences that prepare learners for the real world.

My Vision

I envision a world where language is never a barrier to someone's dreams, opportunities, or potential.

Through Skylingoo, I aim to create confident individuals who don't just speak English but communicate with clarity, express their thoughts fearlessly, and leave a lasting impression wherever they go.

Because confidence isn't something you're born with. It's something you build.
